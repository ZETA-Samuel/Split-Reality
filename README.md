The world was once a single peaceful place. However, an unknown event split reality into two dimensions:
1. The Living World: Bright, peaceful, full of life.
2. The Broken World: Dark, corrupted, dangerous, and mysterious.
You play as a character who can travel between these two dimensions.
Both reality occupy the same space.

Why I made this game:
The week 4 of Terra had a theme: **Split**

So, my game just follows the theme.
How?
Well, it consisits of the same place "SPLIT" into 2 dimensions.
You can transition between them by pressing 'Q'.

<h1>How to Play?</h1>
A / D or arrow keys : Left and Right
SPACE: Jump
Q : Change dimension
R : Restart

**Tips**: 
1) If a level seems impossible, try changing dimension using Q.
2) Bridges are usually a safe place to switch dimension.
3) Checkpoints are a life-saver (literally :>)

You can copy the code and make any changes as you please.
